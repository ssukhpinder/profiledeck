# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ProfileDeck contributors, maintainers, and participants are committed to a
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports the community include:

- Showing empathy and respect for different experiences and viewpoints.
- Giving specific, constructive technical feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for users and the community.
- Protecting private, security-sensitive, and personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Pressuring contributors to disclose identity, employer, account, or profile
  information.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
community spaces, and when representing ProfileDeck publicly.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/ssukhpinder). Do not put
sensitive personal details in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and privately, may remove or edit
comments and contributions, and may issue warnings or temporary or permanent
bans when necessary. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an
enforcement process is prohibited.
